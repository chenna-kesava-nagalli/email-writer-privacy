Email Writer Assistant: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

What the extension does

Email Writer Assistant adds a tone picker and an "AI Reply" button to the Gmail reply box. When you click "AI Reply", it writes a draft reply to the email you are reading.

What data is used

Only when you click "AI Reply", the extension reads the text of the email you are replying to, together with the tone you selected, and sends them to this extension's server. The server forwards them to Google's Gemini API to generate the draft, and returns the draft to your browser, where it is inserted into the reply box.

The extension also stores your last selected tone (for example "Friendly") in your browser's local extension storage, so it is remembered next time.

What is not done with your data

  • The server does not save email text or generated replies to a database or file, and does not write them to its logs.
  • Your data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used for credit or lending decisions.
  • The extension does not read your email unless you click "AI Reply", and it does not read your Google account, contacts, passwords or browsing history.
  • Humans do not read your email content, except where needed for security, to comply with law, or with your consent.

Third parties

The email text is processed by Google's Gemini API under Google's own terms and privacy policy. The server runs on Render. Please do not use the extension with emails that contain information you are not willing to send to these services.

Limited use

The use of information received from Gmail is limited to providing the single purpose described above (generating a reply draft that you request), and adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and your choices

Email text is processed only to answer your request and is not retained by this extension's server. To remove the saved tone setting, remove the extension from your browser.
